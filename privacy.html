Privacy Policy

1 RUPEE KAAM KA

This policy explains how 1 RUPEE KAAM KA handles information when you use this website.

Information entered into tools

Calculator inputs are processed in your browser. The current prototype does not send the values you enter in the calculators to our server. The Daily Checklist uses local browser storage on your device.

Payments

If paid access is enabled, payment processing will be handled by Razorpay or another clearly identified payment provider. We do not ask you to share your UPI PIN, card CVV, password or OTP with us. The payment provider may process payment and transaction information under its own privacy policy.

Technical information

The hosting provider may process basic technical information such as request logs, browser details and IP address to deliver and protect the website. We do not currently describe this as an analytics or advertising service.

Your choices and requests

You can clear locally stored checklist data through your browser's site-data settings. For privacy questions or requests, contact us using the Contact Us page.

Changes

We may update this policy when the website's features or data practices change. The updated version will be published on this page.
