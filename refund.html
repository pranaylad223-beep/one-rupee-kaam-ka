Refund & Cancellation Policy

1 RUPEE KAAM KA

This policy applies to payments for access to 1 RUPEE KAAM KA, if and when paid access is enabled.

Duplicate or failed payments

If you are charged more than once for the same purchase, or your payment succeeds but access is not provided, contact us with the payment reference. We will investigate and, where a duplicate charge or confirmed access failure is established, arrange an appropriate refund or access correction.

Refund requests

Submit refund requests within 7 days of the transaction, explaining the issue and including the payment reference. We will review each request and respond within 5–7 business days. Approved refunds will be processed to the original payment method; the time taken for the funds to appear depends on the payment provider and bank.

Change of mind

Because this is intended to be a low-cost digital service with immediate access, change-of-mind requests after successful access may not be eligible for a refund, except where applicable law requires otherwise.

How to request help

Use the Contact Us page. Never share your UPI PIN, card CVV, password or OTP.
