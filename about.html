1 RUPEE KAAM KA

1 RUPEE KAAM KA is a simple collection of everyday utility tools designed to help people make routine calculations and organize small tasks in one place.

What the website offers

  • EMI and monthly budget calculators
  • Discount and bill-splitting calculators
  • Smart shopping and GST calculators
  • Age and date, salary and electricity-cost estimators
  • A daily checklist

Our approach

We aim to keep the tools simple and easy to use on mobile devices. Calculations are performed in your browser wherever possible. Results are estimates and should be checked before making important financial, tax, employment or purchase decisions.

Any paid-access offer will be displayed clearly before a customer makes a payment. Access is subject to the Terms & Conditions.
